Interested in pricing details? Check out the pricing page to compare plans.

Docker plans attach to a personal account, an organization, or a specific product. Personal plans (Docker Personal and Docker Pro) upgrade an individual account. Organization plans (Docker Team and Docker Business) upgrade an organization. Product add-ons add usage or features without changing that account's Docker plan type: Gordon increases usage on a personal account, and DHI, AI Governance, and DVP add product features on an organization.

Topping up extends a plan's entitlements, such as seats, licenses, or minutes, without changing the plan type. For the units you can top up, see Top up your plan.

Each plan page covers usage entitlements, billing cycle, and management options. To add or upgrade a plan, see Manage plans.
